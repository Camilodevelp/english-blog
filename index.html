2. Simple Past (Pasado Simple)

Evidence: Audio Script

Used to talk about completed actions in the past.

I _____ at Concentrix for three years.
a) worked
b) work
c) am working
I _____ born and raised in Bogotá.
a) were
b) am
c) was

a) is
b) are
c) were

3. Present Perfect (Presente Perfecto)

Evidence: Cover Letter

Used for actions that started in the past and continue or have relevance now.

I _____ developed different projects.
a) has
b) had
c) have
My skills _____ over time.
a) have improved
b) has improved
c) had improved
I _____ new programming tools.
a) has learned
b) had learned
c) have learned
